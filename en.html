Keep your books in gold

Inflation lies to your books. Last year's 100,000 and today's 100,000 are not the same money — but in a ledger they look the same.

Miskal converts each entry into fine gold at the rate of the day it happened and freezes it there. The amount stays in its own currency; the balance speaks gold. Receivables, instalment purchases, loans and assets, all in one measure that does not move under your feet. In grams or troy ounces, whichever you think in.

Your records stay on your own device. No account, no ads, nothing collected.
